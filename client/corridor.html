From one corridor to Toronto

A proposed city model for exploring built form, movement and urban scenarios, with every layer traceable to its source.

  1. Now: a real 15-record massing crop and one offline streetcar service day. No calibrated simulation.
  2. Next: connect the Queen/Spadina stops to a small street and walkway graph, checking crossings and access.
  3. Later: terrain, adjacent neighbourhoods and a clearly defined mobility question, validated against observations.

This is a proposed direction, not a fixed approved feature list. Toronto project is a provisional name; no rebrand is needed to review this experiment.

Coverage and limitations

Pinned snapshot, not current service. Includes branches and short turns. Flat local coordinates share the massing origin; the map is not a surveyed rail alignment. No basemap, traffic, live feed, demand model or prediction of actual travel time. Adjacent service days are not combined; DST-transition conversion is not implemented. Missing or invalid geometry is omitted visibly.
